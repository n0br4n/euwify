# EuWiFy Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

EuWiFy ("the app") switches your Eufy camera between Home and Away mode based on the Wi-Fi network your phone is connected to. This policy explains which data the app handles and why.

## Who is responsible

EuWiFy is developed by NobraN as an independent project. It is not affiliated with Anker or Eufy. Contact: iletisim@tayfunmutlu.com.tr

## Data the app handles

### Eufy account login (email, password, country)
- **Why:** to sign in to your Eufy account so the app can read and change your camera's guard mode.
- **Where it is stored:** only on your phone, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore.
- **Who receives it:** only Eufy's servers, when signing in. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else.
- The Eufy session data that Eufy returns after signing in is also kept only on your phone.

### Wi-Fi network name (SSID) and location permission
- **Why:** Android only reveals the name of the connected Wi-Fi to apps that have location permission. The app compares the name with the home Wi-Fi networks you entered, to decide between Home and Away. This also happens in the background, when the app is closed, so the camera switches when you leave or arrive home.
- The app does **not** use GPS and does **not** collect, store or send your location.
- The Wi-Fi name and your list of home networks stay on your phone.

### Camera information
- The names, models and serial numbers of your Eufy devices, and their guard mode, are loaded from Eufy to show them in the app and to switch the mode. They stay on your phone.

### Activity log
- The app keeps a short local log (about the last 300 entries) of events such as Wi-Fi changes and mode switches, so you can see what happened. Passwords, codes and tokens are removed from it. It stays on your phone, and you can clear it in the app.

## What the app does not do

- No analytics, advertising or tracking.
- No account with the developer, and no server run by the developer.
- No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
- App data is excluded from Android cloud backups and from device-to-device transfer.

## Third parties

The app communicates directly with Eufy's servers and with your Eufy camera. Eufy's handling of your data is covered by Eufy's own privacy policy.

## Deleting your data

Use **"Sign out and delete login from this phone"** in the app, or uninstall the app. Both remove the stored login and the Eufy session from your phone.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
